# Banana Shake 🍌

A quick, creamy banana shake recipe.

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe bananas
- 1 cup cold milk (dairy or plant-based)
- 2 scoops vanilla ice cream (optional, for extra creaminess)
- 1 tbsp honey or sugar (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of cinnamon (optional)
- Ice cubes (as needed)

## Instructions

1. Peel and slice the bananas. For a thicker shake, use frozen bananas.
2. Add bananas, milk, ice cream, honey, and vanilla extract to a blender.
3. Blend on high for 30–45 seconds until smooth and creamy.
4. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
5. Add ice cubes and blend again for a colder shake.
6. Pour into a glass, sprinkle cinnamon on top if you like, and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Use overripe bananas for maximum sweetness — no added sugar needed.
- Swap ice cream for Greek yogurt to make it healthier.
- Add a spoonful of peanut butter for a banana-PB twist.

## Serves

1–2 people | Prep time: 5 minutes
